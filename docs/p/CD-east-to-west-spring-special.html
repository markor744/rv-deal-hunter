Road and season alerts for your dates (7)
  • Most Ontario provincial park campgrounds close from mid-October to mid-May.
  • BC winter tire / chain rules apply on most highways Oct 1 to Mar 31, and on mountain passes to Apr 30. Check DriveBC.
  • Hwy 17 along Lake Superior has winter storms, closures and long gaps between services. Fuel up often.
  • Most Banff and Jasper campgrounds close for winter (Tunnel Mountain Village II and Lake Louise hard-sided usually stay open). Moraine Lake road is closed to private vehicles; shuttle only in season.
  • You need a Parks Canada pass to stop in Banff, Yoho, Glacier or Jasper national parks.
  • Rogers Pass (Hwy 1) can close for hours for avalanche control. Check DriveBC before you leave Golden or Revelstoke.
  • Many BC provincial park campgrounds are closed or have no services from fall to spring.

Conditions change: check before you go.

Free spots have no hookups. Fill water and empty tanks at a sani-dump (many gas stations, rest areas and campgrounds have one), and keep the RV's power for lights and the fridge.
